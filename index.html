Serial FDM vs. injection molding

When printing beats the mold

CriterionInjection moldingOur serial FDM
Tooling costHigh: a dedicated moldNone
First partsWeeks, while the mold is made2–4 days
Minimum orderHigh, to pay off the moldFrom small batches
Design change mid-runCostly re-toolingSame day, no re-tooling
GeometryLimited by draft angles and undercutsInternal channels, lattices, undercuts

At very high volumes a mold becomes cheaper per part. We'll show you where that crossover sits for your part before you commit.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, before you share drawings if you prefer. Every custom formulation is covered by an NDA as standard.

Can you produce in Poland?

Yes. More than 220 printers already run in Poland, so EU orders and samples ship without customs.

Which materials do you print?

PETG, PLA and ABS from our own DRUKAR line. When you need specific mechanical, thermal, UV or flame-retardant properties, PLEXIWIRE develops a compound for your part.

What is the minimum order?

There is no mold to pay off, so we start from small batches. Our farms are built for series from 10,000 up to 10,000,000 parts.

How fast can you start?

A test print or test spool ships within days. A new series launches in 2–4 days, and the first batch ships within a week of your approval.

Can you produce under our brand?

Yes. We print parts and make filament for OEM and private-label customers, including packaging and labelling.
